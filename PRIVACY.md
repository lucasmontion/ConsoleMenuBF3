# Privacy Policy — Console-Style Menu for Battlelog

**Effective date:** October 17, 2026
**Applies to:** the Chrome extension "Console-Style Menu for Battlelog" (version 1.0.0 and later)

## Summary

Console-Style Menu for Battlelog **does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal
information.** It has no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything it
does happens in your own browser.

## Who we are

The extension is an independent, fan-made project. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored
by Electronic Arts (EA) or DICE.** Battlefield, Battlefield 3 and Battlelog are trademarks of Electronic Arts.

## What the extension does

It displays Battlelog (`battlelog.battlefield.com`), the Battlefield 3 website, as a console-style menu.
To do that it reads information from Battlelog **using your existing Battlelog sign-in**, in the same way
Battlelog's own pages do.

## Information the extension uses

While you have Battlelog open, the extension reads, from Battlelog only:

- your soldier name, rank, stats, ribbons, medals, assignments, unlocks, dog tags, loadouts and recent activity;
- your Battlelog friends list, their online status, game invites you receive and friend requests;
- the public server list, server details and the players on a server.

This information is used **only to display it to you** in the menu and to perform actions you ask for.
It stays in your browser's memory while the page is open and is **never written to disk by the extension
and never sent to the developer or to any third party.**

The extension **never sees or handles your password** or any payment information. You sign in to
Battlelog yourself, on Battlelog's own pages.

## Information stored on your device

The extension stores only the following, locally in your browser:

| Data | Purpose | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Menu settings (sounds on/off, music volume, intro on/off, show menu on start, mirror artwork, server-browser filters) | Remember your choices | The extension's own storage in your browser (deleted when you remove the extension) |
| Whether you hid the menu (Alt+M) | Keep the menu hidden in that tab | Session storage (deleted when the tab closes) |
| Whether the intro already played | Play the intro only once per tab | Session storage (deleted when the tab closes) |
| The menu's background picture (Battlefield 3 artwork from EA's server) | Avoid downloading it every time | The extension's own cache (refreshed every 30 days) |

None of this is personal information, and none of it leaves your device.

## Network requests

The extension contacts only:

- **battlelog.battlefield.com**: to read the information listed above and, **only when you choose to**,
  to perform Battlelog actions on your behalf (join a server or the campaign, cancel a join, save a loadout,
  equip dog tags, add or remove a favorite server, accept or decline a friend request). These are the same
  requests Battlelog's own buttons send.
- **cdn.battlelog.com** and **d34ymitoc1pg7m.cloudfront.net**: Battlelog's image servers, for map, weapon,
  vehicle, award and rank pictures.
- **data1.origin.com**: EA's image server, to download the menu background picture.
- **The EA app** on your computer, through Battlelog, to measure server ping.

These services are operated by EA/Battlelog and are governed by their own privacy policies. The extension
does not send them anything beyond what Battlelog itself sends when you use its website.

## Permissions

- **Access to battlelog.battlefield.com/bf3/**: to show the menu on Battlelog. The extension runs on no other site.
- **Storage**: to save your menu settings in your browser, in the extension's own storage.
- **Access to data1.origin.com**: to download the background picture.

The extension does not request access to your browsing history, other websites, tabs, downloads or clipboard.

## Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any user data. We do not use any data for advertising, profiling,
creditworthiness or any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose described above.

## Children

The extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Removing your data

- Uninstalling the extension from `chrome://extensions` deletes its cache.
- To also delete the saved menu settings, clear site data for `battlelog.battlefield.com` in
  Chrome → Settings → Privacy and security → Site data.
